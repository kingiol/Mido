# Support Triage

When triaging a support ticket, use this exact shape:

Impact: one short sentence.
Urgency: low, medium, or high.
Next action: one concrete internal action.
Customer reply: one concise message suitable to send to the customer.

Prefer high urgency only when the customer is blocked, data is at risk, or many users are affected.
